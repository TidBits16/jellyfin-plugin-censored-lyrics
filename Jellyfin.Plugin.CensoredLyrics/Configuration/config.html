Censored Lyrics

Lyrics are downloaded from LRCLIB. Swear words keep their first letter and the rest becomes asterisks, so "fuck" is saved as "f***". Timestamps are not changed. Lyrics already saved on disk stay as they are until you download them again.
One word per line, or separated by commas. "yeet" is saved as "y***".
These words are never censored. Use this for names such as Dick, or for a mild word you want to keep.
Leave this as https://lrclib.net unless you run your own LRCLIB server.
